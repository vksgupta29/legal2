Corporate & commercial

We act for companies, founders and investors from incorporation to exit, with advice that fits how the business actually runs.

What we do

Advice on transactions, governance and the everyday legal needs of growing businesses.

  • Mergers, acquisitions and joint ventures

    Structuring, negotiating and closing control and minority transactions.

    • Due diligence and deal structuring
    • Share and asset purchase agreements
    • Regulatory approvals and closing

  • Private equity and venture capital

    Advice to investors and investee companies on funding rounds and exits.

    • Term sheets and shareholder agreements
    • FDI and FEMA compliance
    • Exits, buy-backs and secondaries

  • Corporate governance

    Practical Companies Act and listed-company compliance for boards and management.

    • Board and committee advisory
    • Related-party and disclosure matters
    • Compliance reviews and policies

  • Start-ups and emerging companies

    Legal foundations for early-stage businesses.

    • Incorporation and founder arrangements
    • ESOP plans and funding documents
    • Commercial and vendor contracts

  • Employment and labour

    Advice on hiring, policies, workplace compliance and exits.

    • Employment and consultancy contracts
    • Labour code compliance
    • POSH, disciplinary and termination matters

  • Intellectual property

    Protecting and licensing brands, content and technology.

    • Trademark and copyright strategy
    • Licensing and technology agreements
    • IP due diligence

  • Competition and antitrust

    Merger control and competition compliance.

    • Combination filings before the CCI
    • Agreements and conduct reviews
    • Compliance programmes

  • Real estate

    Transactions and disputes in commercial and residential property.

    • Title due diligence
    • Sale, lease and development agreements
    • RERA matters

  • Tax

    Advisory and representation on direct and indirect tax.

    • Structuring and tax opinions
    • Assessments and appeals
    • GST advisory and litigation
