What we do

Representation before courts, tribunals, arbitral panels and regulators.

    Suits, injunctions, recovery and appeals in commercial matters.

    • Contract and shareholder disputes
    • Injunctions and urgent relief
    • Recovery and execution

  • Arbitration and mediation

    Domestic and international arbitration, and negotiated settlement.

    • Arbitration clauses and proceedings

    • Appeals and special leave petitions
    • Challenges to regulatory orders

    • Settlement and compounding
