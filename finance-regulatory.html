Finance & regulatory

We advise on financing, capital raising, insolvency and the regulatory regimes that govern financial businesses.

What we do

Counsel for lenders, borrowers, issuers and regulated entities.

  • Banking and finance

    Documentation, security and enforcement for lenders and borrowers.

    • Term loans, working capital and project finance
    • Security creation and perfection
    • Enforcement under SARFAESI and related laws

  • Capital markets

    Equity and debt issuances and listed-company obligations.

    • IPOs, QIPs and rights issues
    • Debt securities and listing
    • Takeover and LODR compliance

  • Insolvency and bankruptcy

    Advice to creditors, debtors and resolution applicants under the IBC.

    • Corporate insolvency resolution
    • Resolution plans and liquidation
    • Pre-insolvency negotiation

  • Financial regulatory

    Licensing and compliance for banks, NBFCs and fintechs.

    • RBI regulation and approvals
    • Payments and lending models
    • FEMA and cross-border flows

  • Securities regulation

    Compliance, inquiries and representation before SEBI.

    • Insider trading and disclosure compliance
    • Show-cause and adjudication responses
    • Intermediary registration

  • Investment funds

    Structuring and regulating domestic and offshore funds.

    • AIF and FPI structuring
    • Fund documents and investor terms
    • Ongoing regulatory compliance

  • Data privacy and protection

    Building compliance with India’s data protection law.

    • DPDP Act compliance programmes
    • Privacy notices and consent flows
    • Vendor and cross-border data terms

Where we appear and advise

Regulators, authorities and forums we work with most often in this area.

  • Reserve Bank of India
  • SEBI
  • IBBI
  • NCLT and NCLAT
  • DRT and DRAT
  • Stock exchanges and depositories
